Questions, answered

Short answers keep the purchase path moving.

What do I pay?

The advertised service path is a $4 start fee plus a $4/month recurring subscription. The exact checkout total and terms shown by Stripe control the transaction.

Does the website itself activate cellular service?

No. The site handles the software control plane and payment flow. A real phone number, spectrum access, SIM profile or eSIM download requires an authorized carrier/RSP/MVNO integration.

Can I use eSIM?

Yes where a compatible, authorized eSIM provider is configured. The Journey section can display provider plans when its API is available.

What does “quantum” or “7G+” mean here?

Those terms describe the project's experimental software/control-plane architecture. They are not a claim that commercial 7G radio service or quantum radio hardware is currently being delivered.

Production boundary: payments can be real and verified independently of carrier fulfillment. The application will not claim activation, spectrum authorization, commercial 7G service or eSIM provisioning unless the corresponding authorized integration is actually configured.
