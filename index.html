No. The software can manage payments and an authorized provisioning workflow, but real cellular service requires an authorized carrier/RSP/MVNO and applicable network resources.

Will every phone support eSIM?

No. Device compatibility and provider support must be checked before activation.

What does quantum / 7G+ mean?

It refers to the project's experimental control-plane and research architecture. It is not a claim of currently delivered commercial 7G or quantum radio service.
