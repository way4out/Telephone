Absolute boundary

The relay can execute software transformations and render real supplied data. It cannot override physical laws, remotely control reality, access alternate universes, or turn a mathematical higher-dimensional model into an experimentally observed dimension.

NASA's public catalog is a metadata discovery layer; underlying datasets are often hosted in separate archives. The relay therefore records source/provenance instead of claiming universal access.
