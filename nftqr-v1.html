Buyer rights + anti-copy design

Each edition gets a unique secret redemption credential, unique ID and one-time server redemption. This makes the edition unique and copy-resistant at the application layer; no QR technology can truthfully guarantee that an image itself cannot be copied. Buyer rights cover the purchased custom edition data/design license stated at checkout; StellarNet marks, software, infrastructure and third-party rights remain separate unless a written agreement says otherwise.

On-chain / Bankr path

The QR itself can resolve to a one-time redemption endpoint. For NFT minting, use a deployed ERC-721 collection with tokenURI metadata, then execute the mint through an authorized Bankr account/API. Bankr documents NFT minting on Base; this page does not pretend an NFT was minted without a real mint transaction.
