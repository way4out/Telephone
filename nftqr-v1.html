Buyer rights + anti-copy design
Each edition gets a unique secret redemption credential, unique ID and one-time server redemption. The art layer supports pencil/pen-style instructions and per-cell data, while required QR function patterns remain protected for scan reliability. This makes the edition unique and copy-resistant at the application layer; no QR technology can truthfully guarantee that an image itself cannot be copied. Buyer rights cover the purchased custom edition data/design license stated at checkout; StellarNet marks, software, infrastructure and third-party rights remain separate unless a written agreement says otherwise.


On-chain / Bankr path
